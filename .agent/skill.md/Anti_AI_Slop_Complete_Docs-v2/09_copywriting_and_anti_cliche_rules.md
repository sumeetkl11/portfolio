# 09. Anti-Slop Copywriting & Content Guardrails

## 1. What is it & Why it exists
**What:** Strict copy guidelines to prevent generic AI marketing buzzwords.
**Why:** Premium UI paired with words like "Supercharge your workflow" immediately exposes a project as an AI template.

## 2. Banned Words & Replacements
| Banned AI Buzzword | Replace With |
| :--- | :--- |
| *Supercharge / Revolutionize* | Build, launch, optimize, run |
| *Seamless integration* | Works with your current tools |
| *Delve into / Tapestry* | Explore, understand, look at |
| *Game-changer / Next-gen* | Faster, simpler, reliable |
| *Empower / Unleash* | Lets you, enables you to |

## 3. Headline Formulas That Work
- **Formula A (Direct & Concrete):** What it is + What problem it eliminates.  
  *Bad:* "Revolutionizing the future of team collaboration."  
  *Good:* "Bug tracking that gets out of your way."
- **Formula B (Punchy Two-Word Stems):**  
  *Examples:* "Built for speed." / "Precision first."
- **Sub-headline Rule:** Limit sub-headlines to exactly two short sentences describing real functionality.
